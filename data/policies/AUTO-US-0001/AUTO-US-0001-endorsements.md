The following optional endorsements are fictional examples and apply only if shown as included on the declarations page.

## `ROADSIDE-100`

Roadside assistance provides up to USD 100 per service event for towing, battery assistance, flat-tire assistance or lockout assistance. It does not cover routine maintenance or repeated service requests caused by an unrepaired mechanical condition.

## `RENTAL-40`

Rental transportation reimbursement provides up to USD 40 per day for a maximum of 30 days when the covered vehicle is unavailable because of a covered collision or comprehensive loss. This endorsement is not shown as included on the current declarations page.

## `CUSTOM-EQUIP-2500`

Custom equipment coverage provides up to USD 2,500 for listed permanently installed equipment after a covered loss. Unlisted equipment is not covered under this fictional endorsement. This endorsement is not shown as included on the current declarations page.

No endorsement changes the synthetic jurisdiction, policy period, currency or declared vehicle without a separately recorded policy amendment.
